How to read this

A rate is how many times a word appears in every 10,000 words of a genre. A word is shown only if it appears in at least 3 plays and at least 30 times, so a name from one play cannot win. Words are ranked by the ratio of the two rates, with 0.5 added to each count so a word that one genre never says does not divide by zero. The grouping of plays into genres is a choice made for this lab. Character names can still pass the filter when the same word is also used elsewhere: bottom is a character in one comedy and a plain word in three other plays.
